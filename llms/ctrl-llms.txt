# Ctrl

> Ctrl (Ctrl, Inc. / Get Ctrl Ltd) was an AI-powered workspace for revenue and sales teams
> that consolidated CRM, email, calendar and messaging into a single actionable view.
> Founded in 2021 by Omri Sagzan and Aviv Nahum, based in Tel Aviv and London, backed by a
> $9M seed round led by LocalGlobe and Earlybird, and acquired by Sana Labs on 2024-10-30.
> Ctrl consumed other companies' APIs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Workspace, Slack); it
> never published a developer API of its own.

## Status

- Product status: absorbed into Sana Labs (2024-10-30). The marketing site is a live
  archive; the application host app.getctrl.co is no longer operated by Ctrl and serves
  unrelated third-party content. Do not send users there.
- API surface: none. No OpenAPI, no GraphQL, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no
  webhooks, no SDKs, no developer portal, no /.well-known/ documents. Verified by probe
  on 2026-08-13 against www.getctrl.co, getctrl.co and app.getctrl.co; api.getctrl.co
  does not resolve.
